## 3. Hidden Costs Checklist (The "Gotchas")
*Don't just look at the sticker price. These items can add 30% to your total cost.*

- [ ] **Helium Fill**: Does the quote include the initial 100% helium fill? (Cost: $20k-$40k)
- [ ] **Cold Head**: Is the cold head coverage included in the warranty? (Replacement: $8k-$12k)
- [ ] **Installation**: Is "installation" just physical placement or full calibration?
- [ ] **Application Training**: how many days on-site? (Standard is 3-5 days; negotiate for 7).
- [ ] **Spare Parts Kit**: Request a "Level 1" spare parts kit included in the deal.

## 4. Top Negotiating Leverage Points
1. **Reference Site Discount**: Offer to be a "Showcase Site" for the manufacturer in your region for a 5-10% discount.
2. **Multi-Modality Bundle**: Buying a CT and MRI together can unlock "Package Pricing" (additional 3-5% off).
3. **Warranty Extension**: Negotiate for a 24-month warranty instead of standard 12-month (Value: ~$40k).
