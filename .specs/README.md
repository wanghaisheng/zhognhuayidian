# 📘 Product Documentation

**Note to Developers & Agents:**

This directory (`docs/`) is reserved for the **End-User Documentation** of the product you are building *using* Instant Ship.

*   **If you are looking for Engineering Specs**: Go to `specs/`.
*   **If you are looking for Marketing SOPs**: Go to `specs/growth/`.

## Suggested Structure for Your Product
*   `docs/user-guide/`: Manuals for your users.
*   `docs/api/`: API references for your product.
*   `docs/legal/`: Privacy Policy and Terms.
